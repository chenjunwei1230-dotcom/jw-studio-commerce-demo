# Jia Wei — Brand Bible

## Document status

Draft for a fictional learning project. This document is the source of truth for Jia Wei's brand identity, story, voice, and creative direction.

## Creator identity

- Name: Jia Wei
- Location: Malaysia
- Content focus: Lifestyle content and beginner-friendly video editing education
- Audience size for the case study: Approximately 20,000 followers
- Personality: Optimistic, lively, approachable, resilient, and encouraging

## Origin story

Jia Wei spent about a year recovering at home after a leg injury. During that time, she began learning video editing. She slowly started posting short editing tips online and continued posting consistently even when her early videos received little attention.

Some people criticised her teaching, but Jia Wei did not treat criticism as a reason to stop. She learned to process the feedback, improve her work, and return stronger. Over time, the time she needed to recover from discouragement became shorter. Her growth came from continuing to show up and learning from every response.

## Core belief

Keep showing up for what you believe in. You may not know whether it will succeed, but even if it does not, the journey will never be wasted.

## Suggested English brand quote

> Keep showing up for what you believe in. You may not know if it will succeed, but even if it doesn't, the journey will never be wasted.

## Brand personality

Jia Wei should feel:

- Bright, not childish.
- Energetic, not chaotic.
- Honest, not overly polished.
- Encouraging, not preachy.
- Personal, not corporate.
- Resilient, not aggressive.

## Voice and tone

### Use

- Short, clear, positive sentences.
- Friendly language that sounds like a creator speaking to her community.
- Encouragement grounded in real effort and small progress.
- Honest explanations when something is unavailable or uncertain.

### Avoid

- Corporate luxury language.
- Overpromising success.
- Fake motivational clichés.
- Aggressive sales pressure.
- Pretending that the fictional story is a real person's biography.

## Confirmed visual direction

### Direction name

Frame by Frame Studio

### Positioning

A warm creator workspace that turns Jia Wei's editing journey into a visual system. The design should feel creative and modern without becoming a cold software dashboard.

### Core visual keywords

- Creative
- Progress
- Focused

### Colour direction

- Soft white: `#F8F8F4`
- Deep blue-black: `#162033`
- Creative purple: `#7568FF`
- Energetic orange: `#FF765C`

The purple represents digital creation and imagination. The orange represents Jia Wei's optimism, energy, and willingness to keep moving. These are provisional design tokens and must still be checked for contrast and accessibility before implementation.

### Typography direction

- Headings: modern geometric sans-serif with a clear editorial presence.
- Body text: neutral, highly readable sans-serif.
- Supporting labels: limited use of a monospace style to suggest editing timelines and creator tools.

### Visual system

Use visual language inspired by:

- Video frames.
- Editing timelines.
- Progress bars.
- Crop marks and playback controls.
- Episode or project labels.
- File and media metadata.

These elements should support the story and navigation, not turn the website into a literal video-editing application.

### Image direction

- Natural portraits of Jia Wei in a home editing setup.
- Laptop screens, editing timelines, notes, and creative tools.
- Bright everyday moments and authentic product photography.
- Images that show progress and practice rather than perfect influencer luxury.

### Interface direction

- Product cards can feel like frames in a creative timeline.
- Product labels may use concepts such as `EPISODE 01`, `CREATOR SERIES`, or `KEEP SHOWING UP`.
- Buttons should remain clear and commerce-focused, using labels such as `View product` and `Add to collection`.
- Keep the layout calm and easy to scan, especially on mobile.

### Required warmth

The Frame by Frame direction should borrow warmth from Jia Wei's personality through natural imagery, friendly copy, soft background surfaces, and occasional playful details. It must not become a dark, generic tech portfolio.

### Avoid

- Generic influencer website layouts.
- A cold SaaS dashboard appearance.
- Excessive grids, timelines, or technical labels.
- Random luxury imagery unrelated to Jia Wei's story.
- Visuals that make Jia Wei look like a different type of creator.

## Merchandise direction

The collection should feel like physical reminders of Jia Wei's journey rather than generic influencer merchandise.

### Small decorative items

- Keychains or similar desk/bag accessories.
- Short motivational phrases or editing-related visual symbols.

### Clothing

- Comfortable everyday pieces.
- Simple front branding with a more expressive back or sleeve detail.
- Designs connected to persistence, creative practice, or editing culture.

### Headwear

- Casual pieces suitable for filming, studying, or working at a computer.

## AI assistant behavior

The assistant may speak as a helpful Jia Wei Studio shopping guide, but it must not claim to literally be Jia Wei.

It can answer questions about:

- Jia Wei's fictional story and brand values.
- Product materials, sizes, care, and design meaning.
- Jia Wei's stated favourite products.
- Recommendations based on a fan's needs.

It should say when it does not have enough information and should never invent product availability, payment status, or personal facts.

## Open decisions

- Final logo and brand name treatment.
- Exact font pairing.
- Final spacing, border-radius, and shadow tokens.
- How much editing-timeline language should appear in the shopping flow.
- Jia Wei's recurring catchphrase, if different from the core belief.
- Whether the brand is called Jia Wei Studio, JW Studio, or another fictional name.
